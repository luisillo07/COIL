01 Our cultural roots

Two Countries. Two Traditions.
One Celebration.

From Chilean fondas to the streets of Pasto, discover the traditions that bring us to the same table.

CHILE 🇨🇱

September 18 · A nation comes together

Fiestas Patrias

Spring arrives with the scent of the grill, a handkerchief in the air, and a warm welcome at every fonda.

  • Spring in the airApproximately 18°C–30°C, depending on location and conditions.
  • A seat at the tableAsado, anticuchos, and choripanes, shared nationwide.
  • Play is part of the traditionTrompo (spinning tops), palo ensebado (greased-pole climbing), carrera de sacos (sack races), and volantines (kites).
Warm hearts. Open fondas.✳
COLOMBIA 🇨🇴

Early January · Imagination takes the streets

Blacks and Whites' Carnival

In Pasto, near the Ecuadorian border, creativity becomes a collective language, and entire neighborhoods help make magic.

  • A cool Andean welcomeApproximately 13°C in the highlands. Bring a warm layer.
  • Flavors of the highlandsTraditional allullas bread and seasoned roast guinea pig.
  • Art larger than lifeGiant artisanal floats called carrozas, lovingly created by local artisans and community members.
Many hands. Boundless imagination.✳

Signature Drinks

Tropical brightness meets comforting Andean warmth. Find your new festival favorite.

Terremoto de Lulo

A Chilean Terremoto cocktail with tangy Colombian lulo fruit and pineapple ice cream.

Warm Hervi-Mote

A warming blend of traditional fruit hervido and mote con huesillo, a Chilean peach-and-wheat drink.

Alcoholic drinks are for adults of legal drinking age. Alcohol-free interpretations are proposed.

03 / Join the joyful chaos

Main Spectacle & Games

Colossal carrozas, a sky of kites, and a playful spirit that knows no borders.

A parade made together

Pasto's giant artisanal carrozas lead the way, with Chilean volantines flying in designated safe areas away from power lines. Plain, non-abrasive kite string only.

Everyone has a part to play

Try trompo competitions, sack races, and carefully managed colored-powder activities with responsible cleanup.

Powder-free viewing areas are part of the concept. Participation is always optional.

A creative festival concept. Menus and activities are proposed experiences.

03 Big celebration. Lighter footprint.

100% Biodegradable
Cornstarch Powder
& Zero-Plastic Fondas

Celebrate our roots.
Care for the ground beneath them.

Our proposal replaces chemical carnival foam and synthetic talcum with non-toxic, biodegradable cornstarch-based colored powder, using vegetable dyes from beetroot, turmeric, and spinach. Every batch would require independent safety testing.

Keep the journey thoughtful ↗
✳

Natural Ingredients

Plant-based colors. Thoughtfully sourced. Independently assessed.

Reusable Tableware

Pocillos de greda — reusable clay cups — plus edible plates or reusable alternatives.

Reduced Plastic Waste

A zero-plastic ambition, supported by refill points and clear waste sorting.

Community Sustainability

Local makers, shared responsibility, and coordinated festival cleanup.

Our ambition, with care. “100% biodegradable” and “zero-plastic” describe proposed targets, not verified outcomes. Natural powder can irritate eyes and lungs and create combustible dust. Avoid inhalation, faces, flames, and airborne clouds; provide powder-free areas. Keep powder out of waterways, collect residue, and follow local disposal rules. Biodegradability depends on ingredients and conditions; vegetable dyes are not automatically harmless.
